[Here is a part of a documentary on decay that aired on BBC.](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sNAxrpzc6ws#t=3825s)



The part I am talking about is at 1:03:45 and demonstrates a [slime mold](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Slime_mold) searching for its food, optimizing the network path between food sources and even creating some backup routes.



This is quite interesting considering that network resolution is a *hard* problem that requires quite a bit of processing.
